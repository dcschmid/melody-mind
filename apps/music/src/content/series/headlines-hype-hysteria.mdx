Headlines, Hype & Hysteria is a ten-album satirical concept series about the fictional
newsroom Beacon 24 Media. Reporter Maya Reed arrives expecting journalism to mean research
and sources. What she finds instead: editorial meetings that never end, deadlines that
keep moving, and managers who can turn any problem into a PowerPoint slide.

The chaos escalates fast. Beacon 24 launches round-the-clock breaking news, leans hard
into clickbait, turns political fights into prime-time spectacle, and eventually hands
story selection to an algorithm called PULSE — because outrage keeps people watching.
Advertisers start shaping editorial calls; social metrics start outweighing context.

Across the ten albums, Maya and colleagues Frank Mercer, Priya Shah, Chloe Vance and
anchor Leo Brand stop reporting on the chaos and start living inside it.

Musically, the series runs on Classic Heavy Metal, Hard Rock, Punk Rock and melodic Rock —
energetic and hook-driven, built to carry character comedy and a continuous story rather
than push into extreme metal.

Underneath the jokes sits a real question: what happens once being first matters more than
being right? Headlines, Hype & Hysteria follows Beacon 24 from harmless editorial
absurdity through shouting panel shows and AI-generated deepfakes, all the way to the day
the newsroom becomes the biggest story itself.
