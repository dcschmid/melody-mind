# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MelodyMind Reviews is for rock and metal listeners who want to understand how an album
works before or after hearing it, without treating criticism as a purchase guide.

## Product Purpose

The app publishes sourced album criticism with a visible thesis, named authorship,
specific track evidence, justified limits, and no numerical scores.

## Brand Personality

Exact, patient, and independent. The page should feel like a record placed under a
single reading light: enough atmosphere to establish attention, no decoration that
competes with the argument.

## Anti-references

- Streaming-service grids, star ratings, score badges, or purchase prompts.
- Identical article cards and generic magazine category clutter.
- Unlicensed cover reproduction, hidden sources, or invented editorial distance.
- Decorative diagrams that do not help a reader hear the record differently.

## Design Principles

- Put the album, thesis, author, and date before secondary context.
- Make the Review Map an argument index, not a track-ranking device.
- Keep sources, rights notes, and AI-assistance disclosure visible.
- Use one quiet, stable reading column and preserve the complete article without JavaScript.
- Treat MelodyMind's own music as editorial commentary, never independent review.

## Accessibility and privacy

WCAG 2.2 AA is the baseline. The app has visible focus, 44px targets, reduced-motion and
forced-colors support. It has no accounts, analytics, cookies, comments, embeds, or
client-side storage.
