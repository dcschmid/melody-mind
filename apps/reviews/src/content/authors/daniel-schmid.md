Daniel Schmid founded MelodyMind Reviews to make room for criticism that stays close to
the record. His reviews concentrate on arrangement, production, sequence, and the gap
between an album's declared ambition and its audible result.

Research and drafting may use AI assistance. Schmid reviews the argument, checks the
listed sources, and takes responsibility for the published judgment.
