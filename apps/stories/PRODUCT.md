# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MelodyMind Stories is for curious music listeners who want a carefully sourced account
of how records, performances, places, and visual culture were made.

## Product Purpose

The app publishes long-form music journalism with visible sourcing and image rights.
Its thirty-eight-story reading room combines artist portraits, scene reports, technology
stories, and annotated cover stories.

## Brand Personality

Exact, atmospheric, and patient. The reading experience should feel like an evening in
a small archive: low light, generous margins, strong type, and every object documented.

## Anti-references

- A magazine homepage made from identical article cards.
- Nostalgia that turns contested history into a heroic myth.
- Decorative archival styling that makes text harder to read.
- Hidden sourcing, uncredited imagery, engagement bait, or algorithmic feeds.

## Design Principles

- Give each story one decisive visual entrance.
- Keep the reading column between 68 and 72 characters.
- Make sources and image rights part of the article, not fine print.
- Use motion only to clarify hierarchy or annotation state.
- Preserve the complete article and artifact notes without JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the baseline. The app supports keyboard and touch input, visible focus,
reduced motion, forced colors, 44px targets, semantic endnotes, and text alternatives
for every editorial image.

## Privacy

The app has no accounts, analytics, cookies, external embeds, or client-side storage.
