# Design System: MelodyMind Stories

## Creative North Star

The Reading Room After Closing. A deep blue-black archive table, warm type, one
carefully lit object, and visible catalog notes. Pages feel composed, not decorated.

## Color

The strategy is restrained with two deliberate signals. Teal identifies navigation,
links, and active annotation state. Oxide is reserved for cover-story numbers and
small editorial cues. Neither color is used as ambient decoration.

## Typography

Literata carries headlines and pull lines. Atkinson Hyperlegible carries all body copy,
controls, metadata, captions, and sources. Reading text is 18px or larger and limited
to 68–72 characters.

## Layout

The homepage is image-led and asymmetrical. It does not use equal article cards.
Articles shift the hero image off the text grid, then return to a calm reading column.
Dividers, scale, and whitespace do the structural work.

## Motion

The first viewport fades upward in a short stagger. Images gain slight depth on hover
where a link is present. Artifact notes synchronize with their numbered markers.
Reduced-motion preferences remove all nonessential transitions.

## Accessibility

Focus is always visible. Targets are at least 44px. Forced Colors retains borders,
links, and marker numbers. The annotated cover remains readable as an ordered list
when JavaScript is unavailable.
