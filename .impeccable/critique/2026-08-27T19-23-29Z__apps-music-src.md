## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Strongly authored for this product. The "Listening Room" identity is real: editorial copy ("Choose the idea. We choose the records."), transparent curation rules ("Why these records", "Why it fits"), series intermissions in the global player, AI disclosure woven into the hero. Nothing here is category-interchangeable. Weakness is internal consistency, not identity: the type ramp and control vocabulary drift surface by surface.

**Deterministic scan:** 60 advisory findings, no anti-pattern hits (no gradient text, glassmorphism, bounce, side-stripe cards). Two rule families:
- 52× `design-system-font-size` — literal/off-ramp font sizes across 18 files (RelatedContentSection, AiDisclosure, NotFoundContent, StaticPageHero, album Hero, DirectoryHero, EmbedAlbumPlayer, AlbumSeriesSection, FeatureLinkListSection, home Hero, LatestAlbumsSection, about, drive, favorites, genre/[genre], radio, series/[series], visuals). Includes page-H1 clamps that don't match each other (album 4.45rem, radio 4.75rem, favorites 3.4rem, home 5rem) and small literals (0.72/0.75/0.88rem, 1rem).
- 8× `design-system-color` — neutral black/white alpha literals for shadows/overlays in PlaylistPlayer.astro (L333, 651, 758, 762), FeatureLinkListSection.astro (L214, 218, 230), GlobalPlayer.astro (L213); these bypass `--shadow-*` tokens so contrast/forced-colors overrides can't reach them.

Detector missed (manual find): three `font-weight: 500` literals (album/Hero.astro L310, HeaderNav.astro L227, favorites.astro L122) that render as 400 because only 400/700 faces exist with `font-synthesis: none`.

## Overall Impression

A well-crafted, accessibility-deep product whose biggest weakness is typographic self-consistency: the brand says "typography-led hierarchy", yet the most important heading on the core album page renders at regular weight, and every page H1 is a different size and weight. Second gap: the global player hides two whole features (Visuals, Drive) behind unlabeled icons.

## What's Working

1. **Editorial voice + transparent curation** — radio stations expose their selection rule ("Why these records"), mood results explain "Why it fits". Rare, distinctive, on-brand.
2. **Accessibility depth** — aria-live status regions on every interactive surface, focus-visible styles, forced-colors blocks, prefers-reduced-motion guards, focus-trapped mobile drawer with inert background, keyboard-operable popover, sr-only labels, range input with aria-valuetext.
3. **Player architecture** — persistent global player (transition:persist) with compact/expanded views, series intermission, JSON queue endpoints, per-track lyrics/download actions with mobile popover fallback.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Page-title typography breaks the documented scale.** H1s: home = 700 @ ≤5rem; album = `font-weight: 500` literal → renders 400 @ ≤4.45rem; radio = inherited 400 @ ≤4.75rem; mood = 400 @ fixed 3rem; favorites = `font-weight: 500` literal → 400 @ ≤3.4rem. The 500 literals violate the Real-Weight Rule and silently render regular (two-face font, font-synthesis:none). 52 off-ramp font-size literals across 18 files mean DESIGN.md's ramp no longer matches reality. Fix: pick one documented Display step for page H1s (size token + `--font-weight-bold`), apply to album/radio/mood/favorites heroes, delete the 500 literals, then reconcile DESIGN.md's ramp with the clamps actually in use and re-run the detector.
2. **[P1] Global player action cluster is icon-only without labels or tooltips** (Visuals TV, Drive steering-wheel, Clear trash, minimize/expand chevrons) — inconsistent with the radio page's text-labeled controls. First-timers cannot decode "Drive"; discoverability of two features depends on icon guessing. Fix: minimum `title` tooltips; better, visible labels in expanded view or a labeled overflow menu.
3. **[P2] Home composition is monotonous.** Three full-width sections stacked vertically; New Releases renders as a uniform 6-column card grid at ≥1024px (~14vw cards) — the identical-card-grid pattern DESIGN.md lists as an anti-reference; principle #5 asks for varied/asymmetric layouts. Fix: asymmetric shelf (first release larger, rest smaller, or horizontal scroll row), vary section widths/alignment.
4. **[P2] No keyboard accelerators for playback.** Space/K/J/arrows do nothing; power users must hunt for buttons. Fix: guarded shortcuts (skip when focus is in inputs), reuse existing aria-live status for announcements.
5. **[P2] Token discipline: 8 neutral color literals** (black/white alphas for shadows/overlays in PlaylistPlayer, GlobalPlayer, FeatureLinkListSection) bypass `--shadow-*` tokens, so `prefers-contrast: more` and `forced-colors` overrides can't reach them. Fix: route through shadow tokens.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Icon-only global-player actions (TV, steering wheel, trash, chevrons) with no labels or tooltips; "Drive" is never explained anywhere in the UI; likely to mis-tap Clear (trash) expecting something else. Will abandon the player bar after one wrong guess.

**Alex (Power User):** No keyboard shortcuts for play/pause/skip; no queue management beyond next/previous; every action requires the mouse. High abandonment risk for a desktop listener who lives in the keyboard.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile):** Bottom player bar packs nine controls; the minimize chevron is tiny and adjacent to Clear. Mitigations exist (track actions collapse to a popover, player minimizes), but the expanded bar is dense for one-handed use.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent):** Strong baseline — aria-labels on all icon buttons, live regions, focus rings, forced-colors support. Gap: sighted keyboard users get no tooltip equivalent of the aria-labels; icon-only cluster is the weakest spot for non-screen-reader keyboard users.

## Minor Observations

- Mood result count grammar: "1 albums match" (singular handling missing).
- Radio pre-playback state shows the station name in the track slot and repeats one preview cover across all three hidden "Up next" placeholders.
- Home hero ambient uses `blur(2.5rem)` on a full-bleed image (GPU cost); a pre-blurred asset would be cheaper.
- `--font-weight-medium/semibold` tokens promise weights that don't exist as faces (documented, but a trap for future contributors).
- Mobile menu links render 400 instead of the intended medium (same root cause as issue 1).
- Favorites list is JS-only (noscript notice present) — acceptable, but zero SSR content.

## Questions to Consider

- What is Drive mode, and does it deserve a first-run explanation moment?
- Should the home page break its vertical monotony with an asymmetric composition?
